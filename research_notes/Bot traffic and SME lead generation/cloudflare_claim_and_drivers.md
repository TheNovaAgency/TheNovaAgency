## 1. What exactly did Matthew Prince post, when and where, and what had he predicted earlier?

### Takeaway
The post is real and widely reported. On **3 June 2026**, Cloudflare CEO Matthew Prince (@eastdakota) posted on **X**: "Welp, that happened faster than I predicted." He linked Cloudflare Radar's bot-versus-human chart for HTML requests and wrote that "agentic traffic growing so fast that bots have now passed human traffic online for the first time in the Internet's history." He had earlier predicted the crossover for **2027**, first late 2027 and then early 2027, including publicly at **SXSW in March 2026**. Prince himself called the data "a bit messy" and said the exact crossover date is unclear.

## 2. What Cloudflare Radar shows, how Cloudflare defines bot vs human, and the caveats

### Takeaway
Cloudflare Radar's headline is that roughly **57% of HTML page requests** on Cloudflare's network were classified as automated in early June 2026, against about 43% human. Reported decimals vary (57.3 to 57.5%) with the window used. As of mid-September 2026 it was still about **57.4% bot / 42.6% human**. The metric covers **HTML requests only on Cloudflare-proxied sites**, classified by a **machine-learning bot score** (1 to 29 = automated, 30 to 99 = human). It is not "all internet traffic," and it is not "AI agents" only, since it includes search crawlers, monitors, scrapers and malicious bots.

### Cited Findings
**Headline numbers (dated)**
- About 3 June 2026: automated **57.5%** of HTML/HTTP requests to web content worldwide, human **42.5%**. — [TechTimes, 5 Jun 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317877/20260605/bot-traffic-passes-humans-online-cloudflare-says-agentic-ai-drove-575-share.htm); [Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/insights/bots-account-for-57-5-of-web-traffic-as-machines-overtake-humans-data-shows/)
- Search Engine Land: **57.3%** automated vs **42.7%** human (worldwide HTTP requests to HTML content). — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/cloudflare-bots-webpage-requests-479608)
- NBC News / others: **57.4%** vs **42.6%** for a 7-day window ending 5 June 2026. — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/bot-web-traffic-overtaken-human-web-traffic-data-shows-rcna348522) (via search summary)
- Mid-September 2026 snapshot: bots **57.4%**, humans **42.6%**. — [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/cybersecurity/articles/bots-now-passed-human-traffic-113500720.html) (search summary); [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-musk-says-bots-out-traffic-humans-cloudflare-logged-57-4/)
- One aggregator: Radar showed about 56% bots, "as high as 62% over the last week" (date unclear). — via search summary of [Semrush blog](https://semrush.com/blog/ai-agent-bot-traffic). Treat as low confidence.

**Trend (Cloudflare Year in Review 2025, published Dec 2025)**
- AI bots averaged **4.2% of HTML requests** in 2025 (low 2.4% early April, high 6.4% late June). **Googlebot alone was 4.5%** of HTML requests. — [Cloudflare blog: 2025 Radar Year in Review](https://blog.cloudflare.com/radar-2025-year-in-review/)
- Human vs non-AI bot share in the 2025 Year in Review: **sources conflict.** One search extract of the Cloudflare post says "as of December 2, human traffic generated 47% of HTML requests, and non-AI bots generated 44%" — [Cloudflare 2025 YIR](https://blog.cloudflare.com/radar-2025-year-in-review/). PPC Land reports the Year in Review as humans **43.5%**, non-AI bots **47.9%**, AI crawlers 4.2%. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/bots-now-outnumber-humans-on-the-web-and-most-arent-here-to-search/). These may be different windows (one day vs the full year). Either way, humans were already at or below about 47% of HTML requests in late 2025 by Cloudflare's measure.
- Googlebot was **>28% of Verified Bot traffic** in 2025, and its crawl volume "dwarfed other AI bots and crawlers." — [Cloudflare 2025 YIR](https://blog.cloudflare.com/radar-2025-year-in-review/)
- Earlier crawler growth: from May 2024 to May 2025, AI plus search crawler traffic rose **18%**. **GPTBot grew 305%** in requests (share of crawler traffic up from 2.2% to 7.7%) and **Googlebot grew 96%**. — [Cloudflare blog: From Googlebot to GPTBot, 2025](https://blog.cloudflare.com/from-googlebot-to-gptbot-whos-crawling-your-site-in-2025)

**Definitions and methodology**
- Cloudflare bot score runs 1 to 99. Score 1 = "automated," 2 to 29 = "likely automated," 30 to 99 = "likely human," 0 = not computed (which is not the same as human). — [Cloudflare docs: Bot scores](https://developers.cloudflare.com/bots/concepts/bot-score/)
- Radar classifies requests scoring 1 to 29 as automated and 30+ as human. By default the bot/human view is restricted to **HTML requests**, which excludes API calls, images, scripts, fonts and machine-to-machine requests. — [Cloudflare Radar glossary](https://developers.cloudflare.com/radar/glossary/); [Cloudflare Radar bot classes](https://developers.cloudflare.com/radar/concepts/bot-classes/)
- "Verified bots" are only those that went through Cloudflare's verification (e.g. IP validation, Web Bot Auth). Verified means verified, not complete. — [PPC Land: Explaining verified bot](https://ppc.land/verified-bot/)
- Requests with a missing User-Agent default to score 1, a known false-positive source, e.g. corporate proxies or WARP stripping headers. — [Cloudflare docs: Bot scores](https://developers.cloudflare.com/bots/concepts/bot-score/) (via search summary)

**Critiques and caveats**
- Botcrawl argues the 57% figure is bounded by Cloudflare's visibility (only Cloudflare-proxied sites), its scoring model and its public bot coverage, and that human-like bots may be counted as human, so the true bot share could be higher. Its conclusion: Cloudflare can fairly claim "more bot-classified HTML requests than human-classified ones across observed traffic." — [Botcrawl](https://botcrawl.com/cloudflare-bot-human-traffic/)
- The metric is network-level and includes legitimate crawlers and security scanners, not just AI agents. — [MeasureU](https://measureu.com/dead-internet-ga4)
- The figure includes AI agents acting for people, and conventional bots such as search crawlers had already been a large share for years. — [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/cybersecurity/articles/bots-now-passed-human-traffic-113500720.html)
- Semrush notes the measure is bot vs human requests for web pages on Cloudflare's network, not all internet activity. — [Semrush](https://semrush.com/blog/ai-agent-bot-traffic) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- The crossover is on a specific denominator: **HTML page requests on Cloudflare's network** (Cloudflare fronts roughly a fifth of websites, though no source in this research stated that number). It is a strong signal but not a census of "the internet."
- The trend is consistent with Cloudflare's late-2025 data, where humans were already at about 43.5 to 47% of HTML requests. The June 2026 move to about 42.5% human is an incremental crossing of 50% bot share, not a sudden jump.
- Because classification is probabilistic and sophisticated agentic browsers can look human, the measured bot share is more likely an undercount than an overcount, though header-stripping false positives work the other way.

### Gaps
- I could not pull Radar's full time series to give exact monthly human-share values for 2024 to 2026.
- The 2024 Year in Review bot/human figures were not retrieved in this session.
- The reconciliation of the two 2025 Year in Review human-share figures (47% vs 43.5%) is unresolved.

## 3. How other sources compare (Imperva/Thales, HUMAN, DataDome, Fastly, Akamai, TollBit)

### Takeaway
Other vendors, each measuring their own customer base, had reported majority-bot traffic *before* Cloudflare. **Imperva** reported 51% automated for 2024 data (in its April 2025 report) and **53%** for 2025 data (2026 report). The figures are not comparable across vendors: each uses different networks, verticals, units (requests vs sessions) and definitions. All of them show automated traffic growing much faster than human traffic.

### Cited Findings
- **Imperva/Thales 2025 Bad Bot Report (Apr 2025, 2024 data):** automated traffic **51%**, surpassing humans "for the first time in a decade." Bad bots **37%** of all traffic. — [Thales newsroom](https://cpl.thalesgroup.com/about-us/newsroom/2025-imperva-bad-bot-report-ai-internet-traffic); [Imperva 2025 report](https://www.imperva.com/resources/resource-library/reports/2025-bad-bot-report/)
- **Imperva/Thales 2026 Bad Bot Report (May 2026, 2025 data):** bots **>53%** of web traffic, human **47%**. Bad bots **40%**. 17.2 trillion malicious bot requests blocked. 27% of bot attacks target APIs. — [Imperva blog: Bad Bot Report 2026](https://www.imperva.com/blog/bad-bot-report-2026-bots-agentic-age/); [2026 report PDF](https://www.imperva.com/resources/reports/2026-Thales-Bad-Bot-Report.pdf); [Softprom](https://softprom.com/thales-imperva-bad-bot-report-2026-en)

### Inferences
- The broad direction (automated traffic at or above 50% and growing faster than human traffic) is corroborated by at least three independent vendors. The specific "first time in history" framing is Cloudflare-specific. Imperva had already claimed a majority-bot crossover for 2024 data, a year earlier.
- AI-specific traffic is still a small slice of total requests in most datasets: about 2% of sessions (Imperva), 1.7% of requests (DataDome), and 4.2% of HTML requests (Cloudflare 2025). Most bot traffic is still conventional bots (search crawlers, scrapers, monitors, malicious bots). AI is the *fastest-growing* component and the swing factor that pushed shares over 50%.

## 4. What is driving the growth, and is "it's Gemini's fault" supported?

### Takeaway
Prince attributes the early crossover to **agentic traffic**: AI agents and browser agents fetching pages for users, which may hit 1,000x more sites than a human doing the same task. Cloudflare and other datasets also show heavy **AI training crawling** (52% of crawler requests by June 2026, per a secondary summary of Cloudflare's July 2026 report) and fast growth in **retrieval/user-action fetchers** (ChatGPT-User, Perplexity). **Google** is the single largest crawler, because Googlebot is dual-purpose for search and AI. But **no source I found attributes the crossover to Gemini**, and Prince's comments about Gemini concern blocking Google's AI features, not traffic volume. "It's Gemini's fault" is **not supported** by the evidence found.

### Cited Findings
**Agentic traffic (Prince's stated driver)**
- Prince: "agentic traffic growing so fast that bots have now passed human traffic online." — [Yahoo Tech (Tom's Hardware)](https://tech.yahoo.com/cybersecurity/articles/bots-now-passed-human-traffic-113500720.html)
- Agent fan-out: an agent may visit 1,000x the sites a human would (5 vs 5,000). — [TechCrunch, Mar 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/19/online-bot-traffic-will-exceed-human-traffic-by-2027-cloudflare-ceo-says/)
- HUMAN: agentic AI traffic +7,851% YoY in 2025. By Oct 2026, Meta and OpenAI were about 90% of agentic traffic. Agentic browsers were about three-quarters of agentic traffic in April 2026. — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/09/3270682/0/en/human-security-s-2026-state-of-ai-traffic-cyberthreat-benchmark-report-signals-a-new-internet-era-automation-growth-now-outpaces-humans.html); [HUMAN Oct 2026](https://www.humansecurity.com/learn/blog/october-2026-agentic-traffic/); [HUMAN Apr 2026](https://www.humansecurity.com/learn/blog/state-of-agentic-traffic-april-26/)

**Training crawlers vs search vs user-action**
- Cloudflare (Aug 2025, trailing 12 months): **80%** of AI crawling was for training, 18% for search, 2% for user actions. — [Cloudflare "crawl-to-click gap" (mirror)](https://gixtools.net/2025/08/the-crawl-to-click-gap-cloudflare-data-on-ai-bots-training-and-referrals/); original at [blog.cloudflare.com](https://blog.cloudflare.com/crawlers-click-ai-bots-training)
- Cloudflare "Content Independence Day, one year on" report (1 Jul 2026): AI training was **52% of crawler requests** (vs 22% in spring 2025) per a daily.dev summary. **Not confirmed in the primary text.** In some heavily crawled categories (reportedly retail, software, IT, finance) human traffic fell **as much as 40% in under a year**. Cloudflare now lets customers distinguish Search, Agent and Training bots, and is evolving Pay-Per-Crawl into "Pay Per Use." — [Cloudflare blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/agentic-internet-bot-report/); [Cloudflare press release](https://www.cloudflare.net/news/news-details/2026/Cloudflare-Allows-the-Agentic-Internet-to-Flourish-with-a-Simple-Philosophy-Your-Content-Your-Rules/default.aspx); [daily.dev summary](https://daily.dev/posts/content-independence-day-one-year-on-building-the-business-model-for-the-agentic-internet-y7p26qdq7)
- One report: AI crawlers were **20.3% of verified bot traffic** in May 2026, and AI-search bots another 6.5%. — via search summary (outlet cited alongside [Cybersecurity News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/bots-surpass-humans-in-web-traffic/)). Medium confidence.
- TollBit: retrieval (RAG) bot traffic +33% in Q4 2025 while training bots declined, with ChatGPT-User the heaviest per-page scraper. — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/software/2026/02/04/ai-bot-traffic-closing-in-on-human-web-visits-study-finds/4921090)
- DataDome 2026: Meta 46.3% and OpenAI 34.6% of identified AI crawler traffic. — [DataDome](https://datadome.co/threat-research/state-of-bot-and-agent-security-report-key-ai-findings/)

**Google's role (Googlebot, AI Overviews, Gemini)**
- Googlebot was 4.5% of all HTML requests in 2025 (more than all AI bots combined at 4.2%), >28% of verified-bot traffic, and its crawl volume "dwarfed" other AI bots. Cloudflare counts it in the AI section because it serves both search and AI. — [Cloudflare 2025 YIR](https://blog.cloudflare.com/radar-2025-year-in-review/)
- Googlebot requests +96% from May 2024 to May 2025. — [Cloudflare: From Googlebot to GPTBot](https://blog.cloudflare.com/from-googlebot-to-gptbot-whos-crawling-your-site-in-2025)

- Prince on X (July 2025): "Gemini is blocked by default. We will get Google to provide ways to block Answer Box and AI Overview, without blocking classic search indexing, as well." ### Inferences
- The drivers stack in layers. The base is conventional bots (search crawlers, Googlebot, scrapers, malicious bots), which Prince put at about 20% pre-GenAI and which Cloudflare's 2025 data showed at about 44 to 48% of HTML requests. On top sit AI training crawlers (Meta, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) and, fastest-growing in 2025 to 2026, retrieval/user-action fetchers and agentic browsers (OpenAI, Meta, Perplexity). The agentic layer is what Prince says tipped the balance.
- Google is the biggest single crawler and gets criticism for bundling AI with search crawling. Its crawl-to-refer ratio, though, is low (about 5:1) because it still sends substantial referrals. Blaming Gemini specifically for the crossover is unsupported. The more defensible claim is that Google's dual-purpose Googlebot is a large and growing share of bot traffic, while AI-native firms (Anthropic, OpenAI, Perplexity, Meta) take far more pages than they send back.
- Crawl-to-refer for Anthropic has fallen by orders of magnitude, from hundreds of thousands to one in early 2025 to hundreds or low thousands to one by late 2026. The likely causes are more Claude referral traffic (web search/citations) and/or changes in crawling. Anthropic is still far above Google. Perplexity's ratio appears to have *risen* over the same period.

### Gaps
- No source found quantifies the specific contribution of Gemini, AI Overviews or AI Mode to total bot traffic. Google's AI fetches largely run through Googlebot or Google-Extended/Google-Other, so they cannot be separated in public data.
- I did not find Bytespider's 2026 share. It led AI crawlers in 2024, but no current figure was retrieved.
- 2026 crawl-to-refer values come from aggregators with inconsistent windows. Verify on [Cloudflare Radar AI Insights](https://radar.cloudflare.com/ai-insights) before publishing specific numbers.
- The 52% training-share figure from the July 2026 Cloudflare report needs confirmation from the primary post.
